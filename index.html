JASHORE POLYTECHNIC INSTITUTE

Jessore Polytechnic Institute is a well-known government technical educational institution located in the Jashore district of Bangladesh.
The institution was established as a Technical Institute in1965, and was converted into a Polytechnic Institute on November 27, 1969.
The institute has seven departments.
